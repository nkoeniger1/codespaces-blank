Our Story & Roots

Founded with a passion for traditional fermentation, North Star Bakery started as a small weekend market stall. Today, we are proud to serve as a warm neighborhood hub where locals gather to connect over exceptional baked goods.

Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great food starts at the roots. That is why 100% of our stone-ground flour comes from regional sustainable farms. We never use artificial preservatives, bleached additives, or shortcuts—just pure flour, water, salt, and time.

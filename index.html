Welcome to the Neighborhood

At North Star Bakery, we bring warmth and handmade care to our community every single morning. Our cozy store smells of fresh yeast and caramelized sugar, inviting local familes, busy commuters, and event planners to slow down and enjoy a treat made from scratch.

What We're Known For

We are celebrated for our traditional slow-fermentation sourdoughs, flaky butter croissants, and custom celbration cakes. Everything we bake utilizes locally sourced flours and classic European techniques to guarantee a superior texture and crust.
